# Feedback from Informative Ballot CH ALIS Connect 1.0.0

## General information
The informative ballot ([IG v1.0.0-ballot](https://fhir.ch/ig/ch-alis-connect/1.0.0-ballot/index.html)) was held from August 7 to September 30, 2026, together with the other HL7 Switzerland ballots of this cycle. It was the first ballot of CH ALIS Connect.

Votes cast: 10 (YES: 10, NO: 0, ABSTAIN: 0). There were no negative votes.

During the ballot, 8 issues were reported for CH ALIS Connect (see [CH ALIS Connect](https://github.com/hl7ch/ch-alis-connect/issues?q=is%3Aissue%20label%3A%22DSTU1%20Ballot%22)). One more comment from Forum Datenaustausch ([#13](https://github.com/hl7ch/ch-alis-connect/issues/13)) arrived on October 2, 2026, after the ballot had closed; it is included in the resolution, carries the ballot label as well and is listed under [Feature request](#feature-request). No related issues were found in CH Core or CH Term. Here is a brief summary.

Effort estimate:
* **Low**: editorial or a single, local change in FSH, with no discussion needed
* **Medium**: profile change that affects examples or the mapping, or that needs a decision in the working group
* **High**: new design or mapping work, or input from external organizations needed
